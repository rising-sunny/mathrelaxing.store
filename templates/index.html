Our Story

Mathematics is
the oldest art

Every pattern in this collection emerges from equations — not from brushstrokes or pixels placed by hand. The Lorenz system describes atmospheric convection. The Julia set is the boundary between order and chaos in the complex plane. Lissajous figures are the geometry of resonance made visible.

We explore these systems parametrically, curating the most visually striking configurations. We take these intricate forms and bring them to the physical world. Each design is carefully composed for premium apparel and high-quality printed coloring books.
