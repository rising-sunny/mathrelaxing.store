Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 10, 2026 · Last updated: May 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data MathRelaxing ("we", "us", "our") collects when you visit mathrelaxing.store, how we use it, and what rights you have over it.

1. Who We Are

MathRelaxing is an independent digital art store that sells mathematical art prints and digital downloads. You can contact us at orders@mathrelaxing.store.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 When you place an order

To process your purchase we collect:

  • Full name and email address
  • Shipping address (physical Art Prints only)
  • Order details (product, format, price)

Payment is processed exclusively by PayPal. We never see, receive, or store your card number or bank details.

2.2 Shipping availability check

On product pages your browser makes a request to ipapi.co — a third-party geolocation service — to determine your country and show whether shipping is available. This request exposes your IP address to ipapi.co, subject to their own privacy policy. We do not receive or store the result.

2.3 Server logs

Our web server automatically records standard access logs: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamp. These are used solely for security and diagnostics and are not shared with third parties. Logs are retained for a maximum of 30 days.

2.4 TikTok publishing integration

If you authorize MathRelaxing to connect with TikTok, TikTok may provide us with basic account information such as your TikTok user ID, display name, avatar, and authorization tokens. We use this data only to show the connected account, upload or publish content you explicitly choose, check posting status, and let you disconnect the integration.

We do not sell TikTok data, use it for unrelated advertising, or publish to TikTok without your explicit action and consent in the MathRelaxing interface. You can revoke access from your TikTok account settings or contact us at orders@mathrelaxing.store.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To fulfill your order (print production, shipping, digital delivery)
  • To send you order confirmation and download emails
  • To issue a certificate of authenticity for your print
  • To respond to customer support requests
  • To comply with legal and accounting obligations
  • To upload or publish TikTok content only after you authorize the integration and choose the content

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or marketing without your explicit consent. We do not sell your data to any third party.

4. Third Parties We Share Data With

Service Purpose Data shared
PayPal Payment processing Name, email, order amount
Print fulfillment partner Print production & shipping Name, shipping address, product details
Resend Transactional email delivery Name, email address
ipapi.co Shipping availability check Your IP address (browser-initiated)
TikTok Optional social publishing integration Basic TikTok profile data, authorization status, selected content metadata

Each provider is bound by their own privacy policy and applicable data protection law.

5. Data Retention

Order records (name, email, shipping address, order details) are retained for 7 years to comply with accounting and tax regulations. You may request early deletion of your personal data where it does not conflict with these legal obligations — see Section 6.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your country, you may have the following rights over your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to fix inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request erasure of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Objection — object to how we process your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format (EU/UK users)

To exercise any of these rights, email us at orders@mathrelaxing.store. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are located in the European Union or United Kingdom, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. The only data stored in your browser is a shopping cart saved in localStorage — this stays on your device and is never sent to our servers unless you complete a purchase.

8. Security

All traffic to mathrelaxing.store is encrypted via HTTPS (TLS). Order data is stored in a database on a private server and is not accessible to the public.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at:
orders@mathrelaxing.store
